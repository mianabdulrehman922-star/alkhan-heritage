Our Story

Crafting Comfort & Connections in the Heart of Lahore

Situated on Mian Mehmood Ali Kasoori Road in Gulberg III, Tandoori Tea & Co. was created to bring people together over authentic clay-pot chai and delicious comfort food.

By combining the centuries-old tradition of baking tea in red-hot clay tandoors with a modern, high-energy café menu—ranging from Italian pastas and juicy burgers to spicy Chinese specialties—we offer a relaxed haven for students, families, and late-night tea enthusiasts alike.
